RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between Mr. Arun Kumar (the Landlord) and Ms. Divya Shankar (the Tenant) for the residential premises at Flat 4B, Green Meadows Apartments, Coimbatore.

1. Term
The lease term is 11 months, commencing on 1 June 2026 and ending on 30 April 2027. There is a lock-in period of 6 months during which neither party may terminate the agreement. After the lock-in period, either party may terminate by giving 60 days written notice.

2. Rent and deposit
The monthly rent is Rs. 18,500 payable on or before the 5th day of each month by bank transfer. A late fee of Rs. 250 per day applies to rent paid after the 5th. The tenant has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 55,500, equal to three months of rent. The deposit will be refunded within 30 days after the tenant vacates, after deducting unpaid dues and the cost of any damage beyond normal wear and tear. Rent will increase by 5% if the lease is renewed.

3. Utilities and maintenance
Electricity and water are billed as per meter readings and are paid directly by the tenant. The monthly maintenance charge of Rs. 1,500 for the apartment association is borne by the tenant. The landlord is responsible for structural repairs and plumbing leaks, while the tenant must pay for minor repairs costing less than Rs. 2,000, such as replacing bulbs and tap washers.

4. Use of premises
The premises shall be used for residential purposes only. Subletting or assigning the flat to any third party is prohibited. Pets are not permitted in the flat. The tenant shall not make structural alterations or drive nails into load-bearing walls. No more than four persons may reside in the flat at any time.

5. Inspection and entry
The landlord may inspect the flat after giving 24 hours prior notice, at reasonable times between 9 a.m. and 6 p.m. In an emergency such as a fire or a major leak, the landlord may enter without notice.

6. Termination for breach
If the tenant fails to pay rent for two consecutive months, the landlord may terminate the lease after giving a 15 day notice to pay the dues. On termination, the tenant must hand over the flat in the same condition as received.
