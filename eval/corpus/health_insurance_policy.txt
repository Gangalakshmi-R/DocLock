SUNRISE HEALTH ASSURANCE - FAMILY FLOATER HEALTH POLICY SCHEDULE

Policy number: SHA-2291-4470
Policyholder: Priya Raman
Policy period: 15 March 2026 to 14 March 2027
Sum insured: Rs. 5,00,000 per policy year
Annual premium: Rs. 14,850 inclusive of taxes

1. Coverage
The policy covers hospitalisation expenses for illness or accident when the insured person is admitted for more than 24 hours. Room rent is capped at 1% of the sum insured per day, and ICU charges are capped at 2% of the sum insured per day. Pre-hospitalisation expenses are covered for 30 days before admission and post-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days after discharge. Day-care procedures such as cataract surgery and dialysis are covered even if the stay is shorter than 24 hours.

2. Waiting periods
A waiting period of 30 days applies to all illnesses except accidents, starting from the policy start date. The waiting period for pre-existing diseases is 24 months of continuous coverage. Maternity expenses are covered only after a waiting period of 36 months.

3. Cashless treatment and claims
Cashless treatment is available at more than 7,200 network hospitals across India. For a planned hospitalisation, the insurer must be informed at least 72 hours before admission. In an emergency, intimation must be given within 48 hours of admission. For reimbursement claims, all original bills and the discharge summary must be submitted within 15 days after discharge. The insurer settles complete claims within 30 days of receiving the last document. The 24x7 claims helpline is 1800-555-0142.

4. Co-payment and bonus
A co-payment of 20% applies to every claim for insured persons aged above 60 years. For every claim-free year, a no-claim bonus of 10% of the sum insured is added, up to a maximum of 50% of the sum insured. One free preventive health check-up is provided once in every policy year.

5. Exclusions
The policy does not cover cosmetic or plastic surgery unless needed after an accident, dental treatment unless caused by an accident, infertility treatment, injuries from adventure sports such as paragliding, and treatment taken outside India. Expenses for spectacles and hearing aids are also excluded.

6. Renewal
The policy can be renewed up to the expiry date without losing continuity benefits. A grace period of 30 days is allowed after the expiry date for renewal, but no claims are payable for any illness that begins during the grace period. Premium may change at renewal; the insurer will send a renewal notice 45 days before the expiry date.
