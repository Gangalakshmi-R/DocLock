LAKSHMI INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
CIRCULAR NO. LIT/ACC/2026/118
Fee Payment Schedule for the Odd Semester, Academic Year 2026-27

To: All students of B.E. and B.Tech programmes
Date of issue: 3 August 2026

1. Fee structure
The tuition fee for the odd semester is Rs. 85,000 per student. Students staying in the college hostel must pay a hostel fee of Rs. 42,000 per semester, which includes mess charges. The annual bus fee is Rs. 18,000 and must be paid in full at the time of registration for transport. The university examination fee is Rs. 1,800 per semester and will be collected along with the tuition fee.

2. Due dates and late fees
The last date to pay fees without fine is 20 August 2026. Payments made after this date attract a late fee of Rs. 100 per day for up to 10 days. After 10 days the student will not be allowed to attend classes until the dues, along with the accumulated fine, are cleared. All payments must be made through the college online payment portal; cash and cheque payments will not be accepted at the accounts office.

3. Scholarships
Students eligible for the government post-matric scholarship or the merit scholarship must submit their application along with income and community certificates to the scholarship cell on or before 31 August 2026. Scholarship students are exempted from paying the tuition fee but must still pay the examination fee.

4. Refund policy
If a student withdraws from the institution before 1 September 2026, 75% of the tuition fee will be refunded. Withdrawals after this date are not eligible for any refund. Hostel fee refunds are made on a pro-rata basis for the unused months.

5. Attendance and examinations
A minimum attendance of 75% is mandatory to be eligible for the end semester examinations. Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may appear for the examinations only after paying a condonation fee of Rs. 500 per subject and submitting a medical certificate. The first internal assessment test will be held from 12 October 2026.
